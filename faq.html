Frequently asked questions

  1. Question: When can I expect my order/How long does shipping take

    1. Shipping usually takes about 3-5 business days within Canada/USA and up to 8 business day for international, a tracking number is provided with your order which you may view at any time Here. Should your order be taking a longer time than expected or if you have any questions please Contact Us

  2. Question: My package arrived damaged or incomplete, what should I do?/I want a Return

    1. Take Photos! and Contact us: Without due reason/ should the factory "warranty void" seal be broken, returns will not be accepted. should your return be confirmed valid, you will recieve a return label and will be refunded the full purchase amount + shipping - taxes upon return

  3. Question: Can I change my order Before it ships

    1. Yes! depending on how far along in the production process your order is you may change some details of your order before it is shiped for a small fee. note that orders which have already been shipped may not be changed
